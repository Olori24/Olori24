Case study · education operating system

NSOS

A Nigeria-first operating system for schools and learning institutions, bringing administration, learning, communication, payments and AI assistance into one operational layer.

The problem

School operations are often scattered across disconnected tools, spreadsheets and communication channels. NSOS is designed as a single operational layer for institutions, with multi-tenant foundations and room for Africa-wide expansion.

Product thesis

Serious school software should be accessible without requiring every institution to assemble a complex technology stack. NSOS aims to make onboarding, administration, learning workflows and AI assistance part of one system.

Direction

The platform is being shaped around institution onboarding, AI tutors and employees, communications, payments and durable school operations while keeping configuration under institutional authority.
