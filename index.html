The architecture

The recurring design pattern across the portfolio is governed execution from human intent to useful outcome.

HUMAN GOAL→CONTEXT→PLAN→EXECUTE→VERIFY→PERSIST→OBSERVE→RECOVER→AUDIT→OUTCOME

Engineering doctrine

Automation should increase capability without making the system opaque or uncontrollable.

01

Human authority

Automation expands capability. It does not silently take authority that belongs to people.

02

Evidence before claims

Prefer observable state, tests, audit records and reproducible outputs over vanity metrics.

03

Durable execution

Important work should survive retries, restarts, failures and partial completion.

04

Verification is completion

Generated is not the same as finished. Build, test, inspect and verify.
